How to Prepare Your Home for Photos

Complete Preparation Checklist

Great photos start with preparation! Here's your checklist:

  • Declutter Every Room: Remove personal items, excess furniture, clear all counters
  • Clean Thoroughly: Dust, vacuum, clean windows and mirrors
  • Turn On ALL Lights: Every light in every room - ceiling lights, lamps, everything!
  • Open Blinds & Curtains: Let in maximum natural light
  • Make All Beds: Fresh linens, pillows fluffed
  • Hide Trash Cans: Put in garage or out of sight
  • Clear Countertops: Kitchen and bathroom counters completely clear
  • Tidy Outside: Mow lawn, trim bushes, remove hoses and toys
  • Secure Pets: Keep in separate room during shoot
  • Close Toilet Lids: Always!
  • Hide Personal Items: Family photos, prescriptions, bills, fridge magnets
  • Replace Bulbs: No burnt-out lights
Pro Tip: Walk through like you're seeing it for the first time. If something distracts you, move it!
